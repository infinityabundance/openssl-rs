## 1. What this stratum is, and what it is not

Phase 20 is the stratum `docs/RELEASE_GATES.md` §1 names "3.6.4 custodian seal", whose maturity
level §3 names **L9 high-assurance custodian seal**. Like Phases 16 through 19 it owns **no
exported symbol**: `forensics/atlas/symbol-ownership.json` assigns `owner_phase == 20` nothing,
and `forensics/atlas/provider-algorithms.json` assigns it no registration row. Its unit is the
**custodian seal contract** — five contract units, each measured by the court that lands with it
— and it hands nothing forward and receives nothing. It adds no crate code.

What it owes is the custodian claim, compiled from immutable receipts over the finished
implementation: a **maturity derivation** (`RT-CUSTODIAN-MATURITY`), a **receipt closure**
(`RT-RECEIPT-CLOSURE`), a **residual disposition** (`RT-CUSTODIAN-RESIDUALS`), a **substitution
witness** (`RT-SUBSTITUTION-WITNESS`) and a **custodian-boundary register**
(`CUSTODIAN-BOUNDARY-REGISTER`). All five are in `artifacts/phase20/COURTS.json` and pass.

**A passing custodian court is an instrument, not a property.** Each unit records
`measurement_state` (the instrument ran and its control was honest) beside `property_status` and
`findings` (what, if anything, it claims about the custodian property). `custodian-maturity` is
the unit where the two diverge, and §3 is where that is stated rather than buried.

## 3. The maturity finding, and how the seal resolves it

`RT-CUSTODIAN-MATURITY` derives the L0–L9 ladder of `docs/RELEASE_GATES.md` §3 for `libcrypto` and
`libssl` from committed evidence and names the highest level its cumulative evidence establishes:
**L8 (downstream custodian court) for both libraries**. L9 is the level the seal names, and the
court records the gap to it as a **finding** rather than deriving it present.

It does so by construction, and this is the seal's own resolution of the instrument-versus-property
tension rather than an accident:

- **L9's evidence is this stratum's own output.** The high-assurance custodian seal rests on
  Phases 18 and 19 and on Phase 20's own completion and this document. A court that runs *inside*
  Phase 20 cannot read its own stratum's closure as prior committed evidence without making its
  own pass its precondition, so the ladder's seal target is marked *self-established* and is
  never read from the tree. This is the same rule `forensics/tools/phase20_obligations.py`
  enforces as a hard invariant: a passing `custodian-maturity` that stopped carrying the gap as a
  finding would let the pass be read as "L9 custodian seal achieved".
- **So `L9` is what this stratum's own claim establishes, and a passing `RT-CUSTODIAN-MATURITY`
  is an instrument, not "L9 achieved".** The court's pass says the derivation ran, its evidence is
  committed, and its control detected the injected gaps; the finding says the committed prior
  evidence establishes L8. The level L9 is licensed by *this seal* — the stratum's closure — and
  the claim the seal licenses is the bounded one §5 states, not a universal custodian property.
- **The seal is the closure.** L9 is not an external fact a later court reads; it is the state
  this stratum reaches when its five instruments pass and its claim is written down as bounded.
  `docs/PHASE-20-SUBPHASES.md` §1 and §3.3 are the plan's statement of the same rule; the
  register's `nc-no-l9-custodian-seal-claim` row carries it as a bounded surface.

The ledger records the two axes: `custodian-maturity`'s `measurement_state` is `complete` while
its `property_status` is `NOT_CLAIMED` with `findings_present`. **A passing `RT-CUSTODIAN-MATURITY`
must never be read as "L9 achieved".**

## 6. FRF and Gemel: the chain entry, and why it is vacuous

**This stratum owns no FRF-declarable court, so it begins no FRF chain and needs no Gemel
checkpoint.** All five courts stage no `artifacts/phase20/probes/<probe>.{authority,candidate}`
pair — they read committed evidence — so each carries `frf_declarable: false` with its exclusion
reason in `artifacts/phase20/COURTS.json`. `forensics/tools/phase_state.py`'s
`frf_gemel_blocking_reason` derives its requirement from that inventory, never from the registry
it checks, and its documented rule is that *"a stratum whose own court inventory declares no
FRF-declarable court has begun no chain and is **not blocked here**."* Confirmed on this tree:
`frf_gemel_blocking_reason(20) == ""`. This is the same scoping under which Phase 22 — the other
non-declarable meta-stratum — sealed without a chain entry.

No `.frf` object and no Gemel change or checkpoint was created for Phase 20, because the mechanism
requires none. The FRF store's chain status is recorded here rather than moved: the store carries
**21 compiled claims with zero blockers**, the 17 in-scope strata each bind candidate
`openssl-rs 0.0.24`, and `forensics/GEMEL_TRAJECTORY.md`'s `current:` is the **0.0.24** release
checkpoint `K67` (`checkpoint.d4e52315f86d381e54c5c6d9cd0fd7283c73ac5ea35b244b7742344cad3d6178`),
closing change `C113`. The store is **re-created at a release, not at a stratum seal**: the
`0.0.25` release will remove and rebuild it at the new version, fold Phase 20's identity in, and
record the Gemel change and checkpoint exactly as the `0.0.24` release did for Phase 19's three
declarable courts (`docs/PHASE-19-PERFORMANCE-SEAL.md` §8; the `0.0.24` release commit
`1b426a91`). This seal records the division: **the stratum records the chain status; the release
re-creates the store.**

## 7. Non-claims

* **No FIPS validation.** Behavioural parity with the FIPS provider is implemented and courted;
  formal FIPS validation is an external certification process, and the candidate is
  `NOT FIPS VALIDATED` (`docs/FIPS_CLAIMS.md`, `docs/NON_CLAIMS.md` §3).
* **No universal parity from finite evidence.** Claims name exact observed regions; the closure is
  over the finite receipts the store carries, and no compatibility assertion is wider than its
  court and capture (`docs/NON_CLAIMS.md` §1, `docs/CUSTODIAN_CONTRACT.md` §8).
* **Memory safety measured, not established.** `forensics/atlas/unsafe-footprint.json` measures a
  footprint, not a proof; the crate has had memory-safety defects of exactly the class Rust is
  meant to remove (`docs/UNSAFE.md`).
* **No L9 custodian-seal claim from a passing instrument.** The level the seal names is established
  by this stratum's own claim, not by a court's pass (§3).
* **The claim is bounded** by the authority, profile, platform and the surfaces the courts reached,
  and by the three compatibility-root families Phase 22 declared but no instrument observes.
