Stage notes
* PFP-69 is a boundary-soundness win with no measurable throughput effect: the
  before disassembly showed zero stack traffic. Reported as a lost stage, not
  as a gain.
* PFP-62's aggregation only pays off once the serialised AES cost is gone.
  Before stage 5 the width sweep preferred w=2 at 16 KiB (45.75 us) over w=8
  (48.73 us); after stage 5 it prefers w=8 (7.63 us) over w=2 (9.73 us).
  This is the arithmetic-independence delivery the generic carry-save arms did
  not show: it needed the competing serial bottleneck removed first.
* VAES-256 contribution (16 KiB): 7.5946 us with VAES vs 8.0409 us with the VAES
  bit cleared (AES-NI 4-way) = 5.6% / 0.45 us. At 64 B VAES is gated off
  (fewer than 8 blocks), so both arms take AES-NI 4-way.

AES-256-GCM final: 8.2245 us at 16 KiB (1.879x), 1.5211 us at 64 B (2.302x).
