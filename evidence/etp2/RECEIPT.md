## 3. The two independently initialised builds

The tracked recipe extracts the build-relevant tracked inputs from HEAD twice,
at deliberately different depths, and builds each in an isolated `env -i`
environment. Each extraction is a fresh tree, so each has a fresh target
directory; `HOME` and `TMPDIR` are fresh and distinct.

**Scope statement.** The recipe performs the two release builds (A and B) in one
invocation. A second invocation of the recipe would be four release builds and is
excluded by the ETP-2 hard constraint and `AGENTS.md` §6.1 (two release builds
are the bound). The two independently initialised builds are therefore the
recipe's A and B, varied on every axis below, rather than two invocations. This
narrowing is recorded, not assumed: the second invocation is `NOT_RUN`.

## 7. What this does not establish

- A reproducible build is **not** a correct build. This receipt says nothing
  about memory safety, undefined behaviour, constant-time behaviour,
  compatibility or cryptographic assurance.
- One host, one court image, one toolchain, one target triple. No
  cross-infrastructure, cross-machine, cross-architecture or cross-image
  reproduction is claimed.
- The distribution arm proves that the *tracked generator* reproduces the
  manifest-recorded shell digest; it does not prove the historical untracked copy
  was produced by that generator, only that the bytes agree.
- A §22-style post-commit rebuild of `S'` is `NOT_RUN`: the measurement is bound
  to the recorded content digests of the crate inputs, the recipe and the
  generator, and committing those exact bytes changes no crate input. The
  rebuild remains scheduled for the next boundary/seal gate.
- The previous record is superseded, not refuted; it was correct for
  `abb315a01`.
- The tracked install DSO and the OpenSSL authority were not rebuilt or compared.
