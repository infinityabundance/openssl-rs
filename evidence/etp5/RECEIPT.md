## 4. Machinery findings (accepted probes)

Four probes validate when the machinery should refuse. Each is a finding about
the validator, not a pass for the underlying claim.

| id | finding | accepted probe | rule that should fire |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | The validator does not bind an evidence record's own machine-readable state to the claim verdict. An `ESTABLISHED` claim over bytes whose record says `WITHDRAWN` validates, and so does an `ESTABLISHED` claim over a record whose element-7 predicate says `established: false`. | `p4a-withdrawn-o7-historical-accepted`, `p6-curl-element7-closed` | `EG_EVIDENCE_CONTRADICTS_VERDICT` (proposed): present evidence whose parsed record declares `withdrawn:true`, `status: WITHDRAWN`, `established:false` or `safe_compatible:false` may not support an equal-or-stronger verdict |
| **F2** | `EG_MULTIPLE_CANDIDATES` is unreachable for a schema-valid document: the pre-scan reads `identity.source.candidates`, but `identity.source` is a closed object without that field, so the rule fires only together with `EG_SCHEMA_VIOLATION`. | `p5-build-b-identity-split` | admit `candidates` as a schema-carried field, or remove the dead branch and restate the split as artefact rows + obligation |
| **F3** | The O7 aggregate receipt carries no DSO digest and cites a BB log digest different from the current per-cell BB receipt; `check_receipts.py` passes because both logs exist and both digests match their bytes. | none (receipt inspection) | an interop rule: the aggregate cites the cell receipts rather than a second copy of the log, and carries or references the cells' DSO digests |
| **F4** | Two `ESTABLISHED` claims carrying mutually exclusive counter values (`1/33` and `0/33`) both validate and conjoin to `ESTABLISHED`; the counter is an opaque `measure` string. | `p3c-counter-two-established` | an exclusivity rule for single-valued counters (`EG_MEASURE_EXCLUSIVE`, proposed), or a counter derived from per-member verdicts |

F1 and F4 are the same underlying gap in two forms: **the machinery cannot
detect an omitted failure or an omitted side.** A17's expected evidence manifest
and closing reconciliation are the mechanism that must close it; this item
records the gap and does not treat acceptance as promotion. No committed claim
and no consumer verdict changes.

## 7. What this item does not establish

* **No graduation and no verdict change.** `ssh`/`sshd` are not newly graduated,
  curl is not closed, and no `PARTIAL` or `CONTRADICTED` verdict is upgraded.
* **No reconciliation.** The OpenSSH graduation vs `NOT ESTABLISHED`
  contradiction is preserved as two records with their provenance; reconciling
  them is ETP-T15.
* **No closure completeness.** The 27-row element-7 repair note is not a closure
  run; the 219-site machine predicate is not a closure run at the current tool;
  element 7 remains `FAIL`.
* **No safety, timing or cryptographic result.** The OpenSSH cells are
  functional only; no ASan, Miri, dudect, Wycheproof or constant-time evidence
  was produced. `consumer_closure.py --self-test` is an instrument check, not a
  closure.
* **No identity that the tree does not support.** The candidate source commit
  behind `3c3a68c1`/`ee7fa27c`, the authority prefix build, the interop binary
  digests and the curl `libcurl` build are recorded as not reconstructable.
* **No omission resistance.** F1 and F4 show that a producer can validate by
  omitting a failure or one side of a counter; A17's expected manifest is the
  required fix.
* **No external review.** The probes and the re-execution were produced in this
  session; independence is not claimed.
