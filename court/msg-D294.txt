rand: Phase 9's census is measured at 95 exports, and three of D287's units are corrected (D294)

Phase 9.0 -- the plan and the census. No export is implemented and no Phase 9 court
exists; this commit lands the measurement that the subphase plan rests on, plus the
three transcriptions the measurement produced.

The working set is 95 exports, and only 25 of them are rand.h's. The ownership atlas
assigns phase 9 exactly 25 exports, every one declaring rand.h. The other 70 are
incoming hand-offs read from the other ledgers' owning_phase == 9 rows rather than
listed by hand: one from phase 4 (BIO_f_nbio_test), thirty-one from phase 5 (the BN
random, blinding, prime-generator and primality families), twelve from phase 7
(EVP_SealInit, EVP_CIPHER_CTX_rand_key, the five PEM_* file-encryption helpers and
four more), and twenty-six from phase 8 (the four key types' constructors and
generators, the RSA blinding pair, and the six RSA padding functions whose bytes are
random). The shape is the finding: Phase 9's work lives in ten earlier strata's
modules, so there is no src/rand/-only reading of this stratum.

Fifteen provider registration rows, counted by (provider, operation): default/cipher
8 (the three AES-GCM, the three ARIA-GCM, SM4-GCM, DES3-WRAP), default/RAND 5 (the
three DRBGs, SEED-SRC, TEST-RAND), default/MAC 1 (GMAC), base/RAND 1 (SEED-SRC).

Three corrections to D287, each measured against the admitted tree:

1. There is no providers/implementations/rands/crngt.c in 3.6.4. The continuous
   random number generator test is fips_crng_test.c (RCT_test/APT_test,
   ossl_crng_test_functions); implementations.h keeps only a dead extern.
2. The base provider's single RAND row is SEED-SRC, not the test RNG: baseprov.c's
   base_rands[] publishes SEED-SRC and nothing else, and TEST-RAND is defltprov.c's.
   The census's own count agrees.
3. None of the six RAND rows carries an alias or an OID, so the census's
   alias-sequence identity check is trivially satisfied for them and their exactness
   rests on the dispatch-association and subsequence-order checks instead.

Staged and preserved, not landed. Three transcriptions were prepared for 9.2-9.5 and
are force-added out of the ignored court/ scratch path so the preparation survives:
court/phase9/rand_lib.rs.txt (2,185 lines), court/phase9/drbg.rs.txt (4,795) and
court/phase9/seed_src.rs.txt (2,389), with the staged plan beside them at
court/phase9/PHASE-9-SUBPHASES.md.txt. They are deliberately not integrated and not
watered down to compile: the DRBG rows cannot be courted before the framework they
dispatch into exists, and RAND_bytes_ex cannot be written before the DRBG it fetches.
They are also the instrument that found corrections 1 and 2.

The plan is staged rather than landed because of a precondition this commit records
rather than works around. phase_state.py refuses any stratum with a plan, a ledger or
a court file on disk and no STRATUM_EVIDENCE row, and the row cannot be added before
gen_provider_algorithms.py stops deriving state as "open" if owning_phase == 8 else
"deferred". With that line unchanged, activating phase 9 would silently reclassify
phase 8's eleven open cipher rows as deferred and let phase 8 reach
open_in_this_stratum == 0 with those rows never published. The state must become a
projection over the stratum being judged first; that refactor lands in 9.1's commit,
the one that activates the stratum.
