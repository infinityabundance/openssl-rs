Two things the rewrite confirmed, and one it exposed.

Confirmed: the staged field suffixes and the generated assignments agree one-for-one --
`cipher`, `df`, `state`, `str`, `maxreq`, and the rest -- with no field left over and none
the generated code has that the table lacks, apart from the guarded one. That is the check
D306 said would confirm the reading.

Exposed, and it is the next unit's first task: EIGHT more sites do not exist at the lines
the staging file cites, and they are a different class from the decoder tables. They are
`PROV_DRBG_CTR_579/588/596/627/633/786/805` and `PROV_DRBG_HMAC_131`, and the atlas's
sites for those functions are the GENERATED line numbers -- `drbg_ctr_init` at 577, 586,
594, 607, 625, 631 and `ossl_drbg_hmac_init` at 129. The staging file cited the `.c.in`
line numbers, which are the author's rather than the build's. Re-mapping them is the same
exercise as the decoder tables but against `drbg_ctr_init` and `ossl_drbg_hmac_init`, and
the atlas already has both site lists.

Three imports fixed in the same pass, which is what moved the count: `time` from the
platform layer, and `ossl_prov_macctx_load` and `openssl_get_fork_id` from D304/D305.
