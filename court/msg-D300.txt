rand: the entropy pool lands as src/rand/, compiled and tested while nothing calls it yet (D300)

9.2's first unit, and the one part of Phase 9 that can land honestly before the
front it will be called from.

src/rand/mod.rs and src/rand/pool.rs transcribe crypto/rand/rand_pool.c and
include/crypto/rand_pool.h: the RAND_POOL layout with the authority's field order
and widths, ossl_rand_pool_new/_attach/_free/_buffer/_entropy/_length/_detach/
_reattach/_entropy_available/_entropy_needed/_bytes_needed/_bytes_remaining/_add/
_add_begin/_add_end/_adin_mix_in, and the two private helpers rand_pool_grow and
entropy_to_bytes. The module carries ten unit tests (634 total, up from 624) and
compiles with no caller at all.

Why this unit and not the front: the pool is the only piece of the random layer
with no platform dependency and no caller, so it is the only piece that can be
landed, compiled, clippy-clean and tested on the day it arrives. Everything else
in 9.2-9.5 needs the DRBG it fetches or the sys bindings the seeding arm uses.
The module-level allow(dead_code) says exactly that, and the precedent is
src/runtime/rcu.rs and src/evp/fetch.rs.

The seeding half is deliberately NOT here. ossl_pool_acquire_entropy,
ossl_rand_pool_init/_cleanup and the /dev cache are in
providers/implementations/rands/seeding/rand_unix.c, which is 9.5's, needs about
a dozen sys bindings the crate does not declare, and is reached only through the
provider's instantiate. D298 recorded the correction that put them there.

Three defects the work found, all in the tests rather than the transcription, and
recorded at the sites because the same wrong intuition would come back:

1. ossl_rand_pool_bytes_needed CLIPS NOTHING. If the estimate does not fit in
   max_len - len it raises RAND_R_REPEATED_PARAMETER's sibling reason and answers
   0 (rand_pool.c:262-273). A test asserting it clipped to max_len failed against
   the transcription, and the transcription was right.
2. ossl_rand_pool_adin_mix_in's cyclic side is the POOL, not the adin:
   pool->buffer[i % pool->len] ^= adin[i] with the loop running over adin_len. A
   two-byte adin over a four-byte pool therefore touches only a prefix, and a
   six-byte adin XORs bytes 0 and 1 twice. The test now drives both directions.
3. EVP_RAND/OSSL_PARAM units: param_time_t is OSSL_PARAM_INTEGER while
   param_uint64 is OSSL_PARAM_UNSIGNED_INTEGER, same eight bytes -- D299's
   finding, which the DRBG rows depend on.

Every arithmetic operation that can overflow is wrapping_*, for the reason
docs/UNSAFE.md gives: the crate builds with overflow-checks = true, so the
authority's undefined size_t arithmetic has to have a defined answer here rather
than a panic.

Evidence of the landing: the prerequisite gate's language census moves 3131 ->
3140, which is the nine internal names the pool adds. The module carries no
export, so the obligation ledger and the court-coverage atlas gain nothing from
it -- naming src/rand/ in phase_state.py's PHASE9_MODULES is the only place the
landing is visible to the evidence machinery at all, and it is named there.

PIPELINE OK, 85 courts, 31469 observations, 634 unit tests.
