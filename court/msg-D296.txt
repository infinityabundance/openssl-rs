rand: Phase 9 is activated -- ninety-three exports, and two corrections to what the ledger mechanism could say (D296)

Phase 9's activation, and the two defects that activating it found. The stratum now has
its plan (docs/PHASE-9-SUBPHASES.md), its obligation ledger, its court runner and its
row in phase_state.py's STRATUM_EVIDENCE, which is what makes its ledger and its courts
participate in its own completion rule.

The working set is ninety-three, and only twenty-five are its own header's. The atlas
assigns phase 9 twenty-five exports, all rand.h's. The other sixty-eight arrive as
recorded hand-offs: one from phase 4, thirty-one from phase 5, twelve from phase 7 and
twenty-four from phase 8. owned_by_module has eleven entries -- src/rand/mod.rs 25,
src/rsa/mod.rs 17, src/bn/rand.rs 12, src/bn/primes.rs 11, src/pem/pem_lib.rs 8, and six
more -- so there is no src/rand/-only reading of this stratum, and the ledger is what
says so.

The court file is present and empty, and that is the honest shape rather than a
placeholder. Every one of the ninety-three symbols is unimplemented and a probe that
called one would abort the candidate, which is not an observation. The runner registers
no court and prints a pending_courts table naming the four the plan gives the stratum
(RT-BN-RAND, RT-RAND, RT-DRBG, CT-DRBG) and the subphase that brings each, the way phase
8's PENDING_CORRECTNESS_COURTS does. all_pass is true of an empty set and the file's own
claim says in its first sentence that this is not evidence that anything works.

Correction 1: phase 8's BLOCKED_HANDOFFS handed DH_KDF_X9_42 and ECDH_KDF_X9_62 to phase
9, and named the condition under which that was wrong -- "if that plan names a different
stratum, this row is the one to correct". provider-algorithm-plans.json gives
default/OSSL_OP_KDF to phase 8, so the plan names this stratum and both are now phase 8's
own open work, which is the treatment every same-stratum blocker gets. Phase 8's deferred
goes 26 -> 24 and its open 522 -> 524; phase 9 receives sixty-eight rather than seventy.

Correction 2: the ownership audit could only follow a one-hop hand-off, and pem.h is two
hops. Eight symbols -- PEM_do_header and its neighbours -- are phase 5's by the
declaring-header rule and their chain is 5 -> 7 -> 9: phase 5 hands the PEM layer to 7,
and 7 hands these eight to 9 because PEM_do_header derives its block key with EVP_md5
(phase 13's) and the PEM_ASN1_write* trio generates its DEK salt with RAND_bytes
(pem_lib.c:386). Rule 2 compared deferred_to[(atlas_owner, symbol)] against the carrying
stratum -- exactly one hop -- so phase 9's ledger carrying them read as a disagreement.

The fix is the rule rather than the data: rule 2 now asks whether the atlas owner reaches
the carrying ledger along the recorded hand-off graph for that symbol. Satisfying the
one-hop rule would have required deleting the intermediate hop, and that hop is the record
of phase 7 having measured the blocker -- the evidence would have been removed to make a
check pass.

The graph is now also built before any phase is checked. It was filled in as the checking
loop walked the phases in ascending order, so whether a phase's rule-2 answer was right
depended on its number. That is a second, quieter instance of the class D295 removed from
the provider census -- a result that depends on when it was computed -- and it is fixed
the same way.

A third finding, from plan_reconciliation.py and about this very correction: the
crngt.c units record landed and the tool reported plan_unit_record_is_stale, because a
units record is kept live by being named in a subphase table row and the plan stated it in
prose. The tool is right -- prose cannot be checked -- so the 9.5 row now names the path,
and the manifest check it carries is live rather than decorative.

Numbers, all read. Phase 8: implemented 238, open 524, deferred 24, owned 786. Phase 9:
implemented 0, open 93, deferred 0, owned 93. libcrypto implemented 2097 / 5896. Two
strata in-progress, twelve not-started. PIPELINE OK, 85 courts, 31469 observations; the
phase-9 entry adds a runner and no observations, which is the point.

What this commit does not claim: no export is implemented, so nothing about the random
layer is verified. The four courts are named as pending rather than passing.
