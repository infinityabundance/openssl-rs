rand: the provider's seed up-calls and the two MAC-context helpers land (D305)

The DRBG's provider-side prerequisites, and the unit D298's measurement named.

src/provider/seeding.rs — providers/common/provider_seeding.c: the four up-calls
(ossl_prov_get_entropy, ossl_prov_cleanup_entropy, ossl_prov_get_nonce,
ossl_prov_cleanup_nonce) plus ossl_prov_seeding_from_dispatch, the walk that records the
eight OSSL_FUNC_{GET,CLEANUP}_{USER_,}ENTROPY and _NONCE entries off a provider's own
dispatch table into the statics the up-calls read. Those eight ids are the ones D299
landed in src/context/dispatch.rs, and this is the function that consumes them.

src/provider/util.rs gains ossl_prov_set_macctx and ossl_prov_macctx_load, the MAC half
of provider_util.c that drbg_hmac.c reaches and that the module was missing. Three arms
of it are contract rather than detail and the code says so at each: a NULL macname with a
mac descriptor LOCATES AND REUSES the existing context, so the function can hand new
digest and cipher parameters to a context that already exists; a new mac name releases
the old context BEFORE building the new one and releases the fetched method immediately
after EVP_MAC_CTX_new, because the context holds its own reference; and a
set_ctx_params refusal releases the context and NULLs it rather than leaving the caller a
half-parameterised one.

Suite stays at 649: both units are internal, and their callers are the DRBG rows.

Two measurement notes, both about the staging file rather than the authority:

1. The staging file's Section 2 was appended after util.rs's #[cfg(test)] module, and the
   crate's clippy configuration refuses "items after a test module". The 166 lines moved
   above it. Worth recording because the file's own layout is what a reader uses to find
   the test module, and appending is the obvious thing to do.
2. ossl_prov_macctx_load has ten parameters, which trips too_many_arguments. The allow is
   argued rather than reflexive: four descriptors, four out-parameters, the context and the
   library context are the authority's own signature, and folding them into a struct would
   be a representation the authority does not have while the call sites pass them
   positionally out of a parameter list.

Both modules carry an allow(dead_code) naming the landing caller, since neither is called
until the DRBG rows are published.
